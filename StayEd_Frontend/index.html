What the risk levels mean

A shared standard for every learning center

Low risk

Below 0.40

Learner is on track. Continue regular monitoring — no action needed yet.

Moderate risk

0.40 to 0.69

Warning signs are emerging. A light-touch check-in can keep the learner on track.

High risk

0.70 and above

Learner is likely to drop out without intervention. Prioritize outreach now.
