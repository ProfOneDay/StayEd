Privacy Policy

How StayEd collects, uses, and protects your information

Last updated: January 2026. This Privacy Policy describes how the StayEd Early Dropout Risk Decision Support System handles information for the Philippine Alternative Learning System (ALS). This is placeholder content and should be reviewed and replaced with the Division's official privacy policy.

Information We Collect

StayEd collects account information for teachers and administrators (such as name, DepEd email, employee ID, and assigned Community Learning Center), as well as learner records entered by teachers for the purpose of monitoring attendance, performance, and dropout risk.

How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to operate the platform: authenticating accounts, generating dropout risk assessments, supporting teacher interventions, and producing reports for Community Learning Center and division-level monitoring.

Data Protection & Retention

StayEd handles personal and sensitive personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Access to learner and teacher records is restricted to authorized personnel, and data is retained only for as long as necessary to support ALS program monitoring and reporting.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or clarification about the information StayEd holds about you by reaching out through the Contact Us page.

Contact for Privacy Concerns

For questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch via the Contact Us page.
