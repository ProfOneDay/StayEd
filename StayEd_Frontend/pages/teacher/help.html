Frequently Asked Questions

How is a learner's risk level calculated?

StayEd's prediction model considers attendance, module completion, and assessment scores to estimate each learner's probability of dropping out. Results are grouped into Low, Moderate, and High risk tiers.

How do I import learner records?

Go to Data Upload, then drag and drop a CSV or Excel masterlist. You'll be able to review and confirm the import before it's finalized.

What should I do with a High Risk alert?

Visit Early Warning Alerts to see recommended interventions for each flagged learner, such as home visits or remedial modules.

Who can see my learners' data?

Only you and authorized Division Administrators can access learner records tied to your assigned classes.
