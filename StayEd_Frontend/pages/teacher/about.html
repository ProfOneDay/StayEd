StayEd

Early Dropout Risk Decision Support System

StayEd is a decision support system for the Alternative Learning System (ALS) of the Department of Education. It brings learner records, attendance, module progress, and assessment results into one place and applies a predictive model to flag learners who may be at risk of leaving the program early, so that ALS teachers and division personnel can respond with timely, well-documented interventions.

What StayEd Does

folder_shared Learner records

Enrollment, attendance, modules, and assessment results for every learner, organized by Community Learning Center.

monitoring Early risk detection

A predictive model estimates each learner's dropout risk and highlights the factors behind it.

volunteer_activism Intervention tracking

Teachers plan, record, and follow up on interventions, with suggested next steps to guide support.

summarize Division reporting

Consolidated dashboards and reports give the division a clear view of risk across municipalities and centers.

info

Risk results are estimates meant to support, not replace, the professional judgment of ALS teachers and division personnel. Decisions about a learner should always consider the full context of that learner's situation.
