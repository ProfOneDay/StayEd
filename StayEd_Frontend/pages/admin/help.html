Help & User Manual

Answers to common questions about administering StayEd.

Frequently asked questions

how_to_reg

How do I approve a new teacher registration?

Go to User Management, open the Pending tab, and review the teacher's submitted details. Approving the account sends them a notification and grants immediate access; rejecting it lets you leave a reason they'll see on their end.

domain_add

How do I add a new CLC?

Go to CLC Management and select Add CLC. Fill in the center's name, address, and assigned coordinator — it becomes available immediately for teacher assignment and class creation.

file_download

How do I export the Master Enrollment Listing?

Go to Reports, select the Master Enrollment Listing, choose a school year and CLC scope, then export. The file downloads in the same format DepEd expects for submission.

calendar_today

How do I change the active school year?

Go to System Settings and select Change next to Academic year. This updates which school year new enrollments, classes, and reports are recorded under division-wide — existing records keep the school year they were created under.
